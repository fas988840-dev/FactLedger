# Support FactLedger

FactLedger is an MIT-licensed, read-only Solana wallet intelligence API built and maintained by a solo developer (Abdullah Al-Anzi, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia). There is no company behind it, no token, and no paid tier that unlocks accuracy — every value is either verified on-chain or returned as `null`.

Support directly funds verifiable work:

- Reliable Solana RPC capacity and monitoring so the public API stays up.
- Evidence fixtures and protocol-verification tests (currently 223 tests across 21 files).
- Documentation, REST/MCP examples, and reviewer-facing demos.

## Routes

1. **GitHub Sponsors** — profile submitted 2026-10-13, under review. The Sponsor button on the repository enables it once approved.
2. **Direct transfer (preferred for crypto)** — USDC on **Solana**. The single canonical record of the public receiving address and its controls is [`FUNDING/RECEIVING_WALLET.md`](RECEIVING_WALLET.md). Copy the address from that file only. It is deliberately not reproduced here: a second copy is a second thing that can drift or be mistyped before a transfer.

## Safety rules

- FactLedger will **never** ask for private keys, seed phrases, passwords, or signing credentials, and will never ask you to send funds to any address other than the one recorded in [`FUNDING/RECEIVING_WALLET.md`](RECEIVING_WALLET.md).
- Confirm the **network** (Solana) and **asset** (USDC) before any transfer; a wrong-network transfer is unrecoverable.
- Do not donate from an exchange withdrawal screen without confirming that Solana USDC withdrawals are enabled for your account.
- Contributions are voluntary and do not buy equity, tokens, governance rights, or any guarantee of service levels.

## Transparency

Received funding and its use will be recorded in the project's funding notes as it is received; nothing is claimed as received before it is verifiably in the wallet.
