## Introduction
I am Abdullah Al-Anzi, a solo developer in Saudi Arabia building **FactLedger** in the open.

FactLedger turns raw Solana activity into behavioral metrics, an intelligence score, and a risk assessment — using public on-chain data only. It is read-only, deterministic, and evidence-cited: every finding names the signature, slot, and program behind it, and unavailable facts are returned as `null`, never guessed.
