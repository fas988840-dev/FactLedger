## Live deployment verified 2026-10-13

Direct checks against `https://factledger-api.onrender.com` after allowing the Render free-tier cold start (~30-60s):

- `GET /api/v1/health` → 200 `{"status":"ok","service":"FactLedger","version":"0.1.0","dependencies":{"priceProvider":"degraded"}}`
- `GET /api/v1/wallet/<system-program>/intelligence` without a key → 401 `UNAUTHORIZED` (auth enforcement works as designed)
- `priceProvider: "degraded"` is a reported dependency field, not a service failure: `handleHealth` intentionally keeps the liveness verdict at 200 and only reports the provider state, so a third-party rate-limit cannot make the platform tear the service down. It means the price provider's `isHealthy()` returned false (unreachable, rate-limited, or unconfigured `PRICE_PROVIDER`). Confirm the Render environment before claiming live price-dependent evidence in an application.
- The deployed build is whatever Render auto-deploys from `main`; the four commits on this branch are not deployed.
